Directions
1
Boil the noodles in heavily salted water, one minute under the package time. Reserve ½ cup of the water.
2
MAKE THE SAUCE
Melt the butter over low heat, then whisk in the miso until smooth. Do not let it boil.
• garlic goes in last, off the heat
• thin with the pasta water a splash at a time
3
Toss the noodles in the pan for 30 seconds so the sauce clings. Top with scallions and chili crisp.

Tips, notes & learnings
everything I've figured out since — newest first
filter: all · tips · notes · learnings
LEARNINGMar 2026
Miso seizes if the butter is too hot — pull the pan off the burner before it goes in.
TIPJan 2026
Double the sauce and keep half in the fridge; it holds a week and makes any vegetable edible.
NOTENov 2025
Swapped in udon once — needs a splash more pasta water and a shorter toss.
